About

Structured, process-driven litigation support

I'm a civil litigation paralegal with over two years supporting U.S. attorneys through the full lifecycle of a case — intake, drafting, discovery, e-filing, and resolution. My background in business administration shapes how I work: structured, process-driven, and focused on keeping every file organized under deadline pressure.

Day to day, that means reviewing medical records and evidence, drafting pleadings and discovery responses, running risk assessments, and keeping filings moving cleanly through California's county and EFSP e-filing systems.

Outside casework, I write on legal and human-rights topics for peer-reviewed journals — eight publications and counting.
